Why people call me before they renew

Most policies in this market are bought in a hurry, a day before a car registration or a visa appointment. That is how people end up paying for cover they cannot use and going without the cover they need.

I work the other way around. We start with what you own, who depends on you and what you can carry yourself. Then I bring you two or three options with the differences written out, and you decide. If the cheapest policy is the right one, I will tell you so.

  • Advice you can check

    Every recommendation comes with the exclusions and the limits in writing, not just a premium.

  • One person, start to finish

    The advisor who quotes your policy is the one who answers when you have a claim.

  • Claims handled with you

    I prepare the file, chase the assessor and keep you updated until the settlement is paid.
